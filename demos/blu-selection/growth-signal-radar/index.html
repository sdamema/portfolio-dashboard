Transparent prioritization

A score that can be challenged and improved.

The model favors fresh, explicit hiring signals in blu’s target markets. CRM duplication, existing relationships and decision-maker enrichment would be checked before activation.

Human-led automation

Automate the preparation, not the relationship.

AI can structure and summarize repetitive research. Qualification, claims, messaging and every external action stay under human control.

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Company press rooms, investment agencies, selected news feeds and job pages.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Extract company, origin, destination, date, hiring volume, functions and source URL.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Open the source, reject weak claims and check the CRM for owners, duplicates or clients.

  4. 04

    Prioritize

    Apply the rubric, record the rationale and send the strongest accounts to review.

  5. 05

    Activate

    Prepare a tailored account brief, outreach angle and relevant lead magnet or AEO page.
