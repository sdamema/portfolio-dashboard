How to read the journey map
1 · Choose the scopeStart with Spain or choose another country. “All countries” is useful for the global issues view.
2 · Separate the audiencesUse the audience buttons to see the candidate journey or employer journey on its own.
3 · Find problems“Broken links” shows only dead URLs. “All issues” also adds alignment checks and pages without a next step.
4 · Open the evidenceUse ↗ beside an article, on a selected CTA, or in the detail panel to open the exact public URL.

What counts: CTA buttons and contextual links leading to jobs, candidate resources, guides, recruiter pages or contact. Menu, footer, breadcrumbs, external references and automatic related articles are excluded.

What “alignment review” means. The URL works, but the source audience and final page may not align. For example, a candidate article saying “Contact us!” can reasonably open a contact page; it is only flagged when it opens the employer homepage instead. This is a review prompt, not an automatic error.

Three actions directly from the European audit

Each recommendation starts from an observed issue or from the scale of the 71-page review.

Evidence → action → repeatable check
How each action comes from the audit
1 · Broken links foundSeven placements use URLs that no longer resolve. Repair these first.
2 · Two alignment checksTwo candidate CTAs open the employer homepage. Review the CTA wording or final destination.
3 · Manual review is largeChecking 71 articles and 190 placements by hand is slow. Automate the repetitive URL and destination checks.

Scope: these recommendations concern content journeys only. They do not predict traffic, rankings or conversion uplift.

Recommended order. Fix what is broken, define where each audience should go, then automate the same check for future articles.
01 · Fix now

Evidence: seven broken placements across two outdated destination URLs.

Repair the current links

Update the old Spain-guide URL and replace the retired salary-benchmark URL used by Spain, Portugal and Greece.

02 · Standardise

Evidence: two candidate CTAs open the employer homepage.

Match destination to intent

Candidate need → jobs, resource or contact. Employer research → guide. Ready-to-hire need → contact or employer service.

03 · Monitor

Evidence: 71 articles contain 190 journey-link placements; three working pages have no scoped next step.

Flag issues automatically

Check new articles for broken URLs, missing next steps and alignment between the CTA promise and final page.
